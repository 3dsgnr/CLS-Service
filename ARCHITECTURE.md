# CLSS Spatial Matrix & Robot Interaction Standards (`ARCHITECTURE.md`)
## Geometric Specifications, Coordinate Rectangularity, and Automated Component Interface Standards

This document establishes the absolute spatial, structural, and mechanical standards for the physical environment of the **Comprehensive Life Support Service (CLSS)**. It serves as the core operational manual for autonomous maintenance robots, logistic manipulators, and the centralized AGI dispatcher.

---

## 1. The Principle of Absolute Rectangularity

To eradicate the **Management Vacuum (Brynjolfsson Paradox)** caused by the erratic curves and legacy decay of traditional urban planning, CLSS enforces a strict **Orthogonal Spatial Grid (OSG)**. 

### A. Dimensional Consistency
* All living, logistic, and infrastructure nodes are calculated as fixed **Rectangular Modules**.
* **Zero-Tolerance Geometry:** Angular configurations throughout all load-bearing, transit, and structural components are fixed at exactly **90.000°** (right angles). This eliminates vector alignment recalculations for automated machinery, optimizing algorithmic processing power.

### B. Grid Quantization
* The entire surface and interior of the ecosystem are divided into standardized, discrete three-dimensional coordinate blocks. 
* Robots navigate using an absolute local matrix instead of error-prone GPS or probabilistic spatial tracking (SLAM). The AGI dispatcher maps every mechanical path as a direct sequence of rigid, non-overlapping spatial cells.

## 2. Standardized Robotic Interfaces

Every interface within CLSS is engineered to support instantaneous deconstruction into simple atomic actions, following the **Quantization of Tasks** paradigm.

### A. Automated Intake and Resource Slots
* **Universal Port Architecture:** Openings for utility delivery, structural maintenance, and instant waste separation utilize a unified rectangular form factor.
* **Pre-calculated Mechanical Clearances:** Contact tolerances for robotic manipulators at dock zones are locked between **0.5 mm and 1.0 mm**. This standard ensures flawless, blind docking without reliance on continuous visual sensor adjustments.

### B. Instant Deconstruction / Separation Zones
* Objects generated within the ecosystem are built out of standardized component materials natively indexed by the system.
* **Component Extraction Protocol:** Assembly points use magnetic or standardized mechanical snap-locks aligned with the primary orthogonal axes. Maintenance robots execute component replacement using basic linear motions (Pull/Push on X, Y, or Z axes), eliminating unpredictable multi-axis rotations.

* **Path Optimization:** The centralized AGI must allocate logistics by slicing multi-destination tasks into discrete, single-axis movement paths.
